WalkingPacks Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

WalkingPacks is a game that turns the steps you walk into packs of collectible cards. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and what we do with it.

Data we use

  • Step count from Apple Health. With your permission, WalkingPacks reads your step count from Apple Health (HealthKit). The app does not read any other health data and never writes to Apple Health.
  • Hourly step totals. To credit packs, the app sends your step totals grouped by hour (for example, "1,240 steps between 9 and 10 AM") to the WalkingPacks game server. Individual step samples, locations, and other health data are not sent.
  • Game progress. Your step balance, packs, cards, trades, and grading queue are stored on the game server so your collection is kept safe and the game rules are applied fairly.
  • An anonymous account. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account for you. It has no name, email, or password. It is identified only by a random ID stored on your device.
  • Your time zone, so days and hours are counted correctly.

Like most online services, the server's hosting provider may briefly keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request times) for security and reliability.

Where data is stored

The game server runs on Supabase, a cloud database provider that stores and processes data on our behalf. The app also keeps some data on your device, such as your settings and a copy of your recent game state.

How we use it

Your data is used only to run the game: crediting steps, awarding and opening packs, and keeping your collection and grading progress.

What we don't do

  • No advertising. Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No selling of data.
  • No sharing with third parties, other than the hosting provider that runs the server for us.

Your choices

  • You can turn off WalkingPacks' access to step data at any time in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → WalkingPacks) or in iOS Settings. No new steps are sent after that.
  • To delete your account and all game data stored on the server, email us at the address below. We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

WalkingPacks does not collect names, email addresses, or contact information from anyone, including children.
