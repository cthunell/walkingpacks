WalkingPacks Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

WalkingPacks is a game that turns the steps you walk into packs of collectible cards. This policy explains what data the app uses and what happens to it.

Data we use

Step count from Apple Health. With your permission, WalkingPacks reads your step count from Apple Health (HealthKit). It uses this number only to award in-game packs. The app does not read any other health data, and it never writes data to Apple Health.

Where your data is stored

All app data, including your step totals, pack progress, and card collection, is stored locally on your device. WalkingPacks has no servers, and none of your data is sent off your device by the app.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No advertising. HealthKit data is never used for advertising or marketing.
  • No sharing of data with third parties.
  • No selling of data.

Your choices

You can turn off WalkingPacks' access to step data at any time in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → WalkingPacks) or in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

WalkingPacks does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
